EPLEY FORMULA

How do it work?

Enter a set to see the estimated weight you could lift for 1–25 repetitions.

Enter repetitions (1–25) and a weight greater than zero.

Tap a point to see its reps and weight. Drag the highlighted point to change your entered set. At 1 repetition, the plot shows your estimated one-rep maximum (1RM).

1RM = weight – (1 + reps / 30). For 2–25 reps, estimated weight = 1RM – (1 + reps / 30). A one-rep input is used directly as your 1RM.
